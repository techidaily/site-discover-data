While the **CMD Studio 2A** is genuinely affordable (listed for $99 USD on most websites) it does come equipped with features not typically found on midi controllers in the same price range – such as **touch-sensitive jog wheels** for pitch bend and scratching, the **45mm cross-fader**, **back-lit hot cue/loop buttons** and surprisingly powerful **4-channel USB audio interface** for pre-fade listening and low-latency control (Behringer says: “highest-quality 24-Bit converters and ultra-low latency performance under Windows and Mac OS X, your music will sound amazing!”).
