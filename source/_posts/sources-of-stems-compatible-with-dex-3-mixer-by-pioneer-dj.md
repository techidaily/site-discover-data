The latest DEX 3 update just dropped and one of the newest additions to the DJ software’s feature-set is support for Stems, an open multi-track audio format.

![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/stems_logo.png?fit=340%2C156&ssl=1 "stems_logo")

Stem files are tracks split out into four separate elements, typically drums, bassline, melody, and vocal. Since Stem files allow DJs to independently interact with a track’s different musical elements, the creative possibilities for creating instant new mashups, instrumentals, a cappellas, and more are endless. Basically, Stems enable DJs to produce new sounds and music during a live performance. Breath new life into old classics or create instant remixes of the latest floor-filling tunes.
