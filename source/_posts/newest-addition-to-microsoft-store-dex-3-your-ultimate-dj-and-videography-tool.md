## Newest Addition to Microsoft Store: DEX 3 - Your Ultimate DJ and Videography Tool!

[![DEX 3 in the Microsoft Store](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/dex3ms.jpg?resize=845%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/dex3ms.jpg?fit=1030%2C579&ssl=1 "DEX 3 in the Microsoft Store")

Now DEX 3 can be purchased directly from the [**Microsoft Store**](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/p/dex-3/9mz54hzxs1h6?activetab=pivot:overviewtab) for only $149.99 (USD). _[View in Microsoft Store](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/p/dex-3/9mz54hzxs1h6?activetab=pivot:overviewtab)_

The PCDJ development team worked with computer technology giant **Intel** over the last couple of months to embed and utilize some of their latest video (media SDK) decoding technology. Intel’s video decoding technology proved to be a great fit for our DEX 3 video mixing software, and with a bit of their assistance we’ve created a new custom build of [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) for sale on the Microsoft Store.

![mixing with DEX 3 at new years party](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/IMG_5116.jpg?fit=169%2C300&ssl=1 "mixing with DEX 3 at new years party")

The Microsoft Store is a digital distribution platform sponsored by Microsoft. Included with Windows 10, the Microsoft stores allows you to browse, download, test, and purchase a vast variety of software and applications. DEX 3 is the now the top DJ software available on the Microsoft store.

Purchasers of [DEX 3 via the Microsoft Store](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/p/dex-3/9mz54hzxs1h6?activetab=pivot:overviewtab) will be treated with a secure and simple checkout system that allows them to painlessly download and install. As with direct purchases from PCDJ.com, updates to DEX 3 will be provided at no additional charge to the customer.

[DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) is the most complete DJ software, video mixing software, and karaoke software system available today, and we’re excited about our new partnership with Intel and Microsoft that will assist with our goal to bring the joy of mixing and live performance to the masses.

More information to come!
